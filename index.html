Selected work

Work across organisations, cultures and contexts.

01

Large-scale organisational change

Turning a technology rollout into a people-centred adoption programme.

For a digital transformation spanning approximately 120,000 Dutch government employees, I helped shape the user-adoption strategy from the ground up — using end-user research and stakeholder insight to identify needs, resistance and learning gaps, and translating these into practical interventions.

My role combined advisory work, stakeholder collaboration and large-scale facilitation, with live sessions ultimately reaching more than 40,000 end users.

User research · Adoption strategy · Stakeholder advisory · Facilitation
02

International learning & facilitation

Helping professionals communicate, collaborate and learn across locations.

Across corporate and public-sector environments, I’ve facilitated interactive learning for international audiences — from small-group behavioural skills sessions to virtual events with up to 1,000 participants.

My work has included communication and collaboration, train-the-trainer, digital adoption and live-online facilitation across organisations including Aramco and the Dutch public sector.

Communication · Collaboration · Train-the-trainer · International facilitation
03

Youth & social impact

Building skills for a changing world of work.

With Na’amal, I facilitate and mentor young people from refugee backgrounds in Ethiopia as they develop the communication, collaboration and remote-working skills needed to access international work opportunities.

The work combines practical skills development with confidence, trust and the human side of navigating work across cultures and at a distance.

Youth skills · Mentoring · Remote work · Communication

About Eileen

I’m interested in what helps people move forward.

My work has taken me across corporate, government and social-impact settings, but the question at the heart of it has stayed remarkably consistent: what do people need to learn, grow and navigate change successfully?

I’m a Dutch-born consultant and facilitator with an international background and more than a decade of experience in learning and people development. I’ve worked on large-scale transformation programmes, facilitated international audiences and supported young people developing the skills to access new opportunities.

What I enjoy most is getting close to the people a programme is meant to serve — listening, asking questions and understanding what is really getting in their way — and then bringing those insights into strategy, programmes and facilitation.

Increasingly, my work is focused on youth skills and empowerment, particularly where learning can create greater access to opportunity.
